Why me

I built it, scaled it, sold it, and learned what it costs.

I built an online antenatal platform from first customer to exit, and I now coach leaders through the decisions that decide the next five years, pricing, structure, people, growth, exit. Clear thinking, plain language, no theory for its own sake.

I'm a nurse and midwife by training. I founded Birth Beat, Australia's first interactive online antenatal platform, grew it to thousands of families, and led its exit to Device Technologies in 2023.

I know what it's like to price something for the first time, to hire ahead of the revenue, to run a raise, to sit through diligence, and to hand over a company you built. I've made most of the mistakes I'll help you avoid.

Along the way I learned that growth is the easy part to talk about and the hard part to survive. That's the part I coach.
