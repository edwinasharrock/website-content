How I coach

I don't start with your business. I start with you, because a business only grows as far as the person running it is willing to.

That means I rarely hand you the answer. I ask the question sitting underneath the one you asked, push at the story you're telling yourself, and hold enough space for you to be honest, sometimes before you've admitted the truth to yourself. Bring the messy half-formed thought. Bring the decision you've been circling for weeks. We'll pull it apart until whatever actually matters is obvious.

I built a business from nothing, back when it was just me pretending to be a whole team so nobody would guess I was doing every job myself. I sold that business. I sit on boards where the numbers are real and the stakes are too. So when we get into yours, it's practical, it's direct, and it comes from having actually done it.

The point was never to make you need me more. It's to help you trust yourself more. You should leave every session sharper, not just relieved.
